# Four views. One project.

Use whichever view answers the question in front of you. Changes reach the
same canonical tasks, so there is nothing to reconcile afterward.

## Board: what is moving forward?

Move tasks through configurable workflow columns. Reorder work, collapse a
column, or add a task directly to its stage. Column limits are advisory;
they warn about load without blocking your work.

Cards show selected metadata, priority, and checklist progress. Checking the
last checklist step moves a task into the configured completion stage.
Reopening a step brings the task back to an active stage.

## Timeline: when does it happen?

Set start and due dates, move or resize bars, and inspect milestones,
dependencies, and parent-linked task hierarchy. Day, week, month, and quarter
zoom let you change the planning scale.

The Timeline displays a window of up to two years so distant dates do not
slow down the view. Use **Earlier**, **Later**, or a focus date to move the
window. **Show scheduled dates** helps you find work outside the current
window. Tasks and their stored dates remain unchanged by navigation.

The working-day configuration controls whether non-working days are shown.
Unscheduled tasks remain visible; **Schedule** opens their details to set dates.

Dependencies signal relationships and blocked work. Moving a predecessor does
not automatically reschedule its successors. Child tasks do not roll their
progress, dates, or effort up into a parent.

## Matrix: what deserves your attention?

The Eisenhower Matrix separates urgency from importance:

| | Important | Not important |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Urgent | Do first | Delegate |
| Not urgent | Schedule | Eliminate |

Move a task between quadrants to change its classification. Priority and
workflow status remain separate. There is no independent manual order inside
a quadrant. Quadrant summaries total the estimated hours of visible tasks.

Completed tasks are hidden by default. **Show completed** enables the project
preference and shared completion filter together. Turning it off hides them
from Matrix without changing other views' shared completion filter.

## List: what needs a closer look?

Compare task status, priority, quadrant, assignee, dates, and progress. Sort by
manual position, priority, due date, or title. Select multiple rows to change
status or priority, complete work, or archive tasks together.

Dragging a row returns the List to manual order and changes the same Board
position. In narrow windows, scroll horizontally to reach all fields.

## A workspace that fits

Choose light, dark, or System theme. System follows live OS appearance changes.
Use compact density for denser review or comfortable density for more space.
Reduced-motion preferences shorten or remove spatial animation while keeping
focus and destination cues.
